Moray Game Jam Sponsorship

Find out how you can support the Moray Game Jam below

Known as "The best game jam in Scotland” Moray Game Jam is now in its 11th year. Hosted at UHI Moray, this 48-hour event connects communities with the gaming industry and attracts participants from across Scotland. With the goal to create a video or board game, inspired by a surprise theme revealed at the start, Moray Game Jam is a celebration of creativity, innovation, and collaboration. Designed to be fully inclusive, the event welcomes both beginners and seasoned developers, with expert support available throughout.

To keep the event accessible and ensure the best possible experience for participants, we rely on the generosity of sponsors and supporters. By sponsoring Moray Game Jam, you'll be helping to support emerging talent, encourage skills development and contribute to an event that showcases creativity, innovation and digital talent in Moray.

  • Bronze - £250 Inclusion in media releases, Logo on promotional materials
  • Silver - £500 Bronze package plus: Dedicated social media shoutout, Own banner on display across the weekend
  • Gold - £1000 Silver Package plus: Guest access across the weekend, Marketplace stall at the gaming playground
