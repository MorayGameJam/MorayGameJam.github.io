Real-World Game Development: Challenges, Decisions, Lessons

Running a game studio is a series of decisions, and some of them only make sense in hindsight. This workshop is an open, interactive discussion about the real challenges, decisions, and hard-won lessons that come with running a games studio.

Read More:

We'll dig into what goes wrong, what unexpectedly works, and how small choices can quietly snowball into major consequences (both good and bad).

Your questions are actively encouraged throughout. Whether you're curious about setting up a studio, running a business, transitioning from developer to leader, or what it actually means to own and manage a team, bring it all to the table.

The conversation will follow what you want to know, not a fixed agenda. Rather than a scripted talk, this workshop is shaped by the questions and topics the audience bring.

Audio Design and Music in Games

Daniel Morrison Neil is a musician, sound designer and lecturer in sound in the School of Innovation & Technology.

Read More:

Daniel is a director at the video game studio Team FOLON and previously worked on Fallout: London, as well as working as a dubbing mixer for various international films, and a recording mixer and engineer for La Chunky Studios in Glasgow.

His main interests centre on temporality in sound design and music, as well as interactive design utilising game engines for sound exploration.

How to make games long-term - lessons from 5 years of solo dev

Iona Ferguson is a lecturer in Immersive Systems Design at The Glasgow School of Art where she teaches: game design, coding and audio design and is a researcher investigating the experiences of LGBTQ+ people in computer science.

Read More:

She operates Arboreta Games where she has released three games - The Black Iris (2021), It Sleeps Below the Haar (2024) and Unto the Aurora (2025) and closed caption plug-in CaptionAssist3D.

She also runs TRANS//CODED, a Glasgow-based organisation offering events and classes to support coding education in the trans community.
